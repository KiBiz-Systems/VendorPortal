# Lesson Review

Candidate lessons live under `ai-workspace/lesson-candidates/`. Verify that a candidate records
repeated evidence, scope, a proposed change, and expected token/maintenance cost. A human must
explicitly accept or reject it. On acceptance, update `LESSONS.md` first, then the corresponding
rule or skill. Never auto-promote a candidate.
